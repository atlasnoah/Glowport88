# Development roadmap

1. Establish a playable baseline, browser regression tests, backup, and incremental Git history.
2. Ship an original material/prop/character/vehicle atlas and a separate art renderer; transform city surfaces and architecture.
3. Integrate fixture lighting, rain reflections, and day/night atmosphere; inspect actual browser screenshots.
4. Refine directional sprite animation and distinct room identities; preserve interactions.
5. Expand investigations and evidence objectives, dialogue presentation, activities, and mission failure/retry handling.
6. Improve collision/traffic/NPC edge cases and profile representative play.
7. Export a browser-ready ZIP and record tested versus unfinished work.

Preserve scale, connected roads, all districts, save key, and the Vee/Meridian campaign. Keep a playable commit after each substantial stage. Do not claim all systems are rewritten or a ten-hour session has elapsed.
