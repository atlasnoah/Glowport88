## World systems

- Context selection uses district, distance, story act, time windows, cooldown, recent-event variety and applicable reputation/weather weights. Forced QA selection still validates geometry.
- Encounter NPCs use the approved character sprites. Accident/breakdown/theft scenes have bounded physical vehicles; nearby civilians and traffic react to accident hazards or danger.
- Blackout repair darkens a local block and restores it after the relay activity. Lawful repair and performer timing stages do not create wanted heat.
- Races spawn two actual vehicles following a validated road graph, preserve their progress through saves, record finishes and pay a receipt-protected placement bonus.
- Decisions and outcomes are retained in encounter memory. Eddie’s completed stock run makes diner meals cost $28 instead of $35.
- Expiration/cancellation clears temporary scenes and vehicles while preserving any vehicle the player has already taken.

Several retained incidents remain short two- or three-step encounters. Their NPC scenes are lightweight shared systems; they are not 32 bespoke cinematic simulations.
